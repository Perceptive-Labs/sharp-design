# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite, React, Tailwind CSS, GSAP.

## Users

Potential clients and partners seeking a design agency for printing, logo designs, and various other creative works.

## Product Purpose

A showcase website acting as a portfolio and marketing funnel to present the agency's world-class digital experiences and physical products.

## Positioning

Rooted in peak creativity, the agency possesses the distinct ability to bring imagination to real life, outperforming traditional templates and generic corporate designs.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive to review past works (case studies, portfolios), assess the client roster (brands they've built), and ultimately initiate contact for new projects via inquiry forms.

## Brand Commitments

- **Palette**: Orange (#FF5C00), Deep Black (#121212), and Warm Off-White (#fbf9ef).
- **Typography**: Striking, editorial typography leveraging serif accents (Instrument Serif) and geometric sans (Space Grotesk).
- **Logo**: The Sharp Design Graphic Design & Print logo must be maintained.
- **Motion**: Fluid, unexpected, world-class GSAP animations. No generic framer-motion slop.

## Product Principles

1. **Portfolio First**: The interface recedes to let the artifacts and case studies command attention.
2. **Tactile Premium**: Digital surfaces should imply the same luxury and authority as their high-end physical printing.
3. **Imaginative Realism**: Every interaction should prove their claim of bringing imagination to life.
